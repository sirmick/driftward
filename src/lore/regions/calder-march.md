# The Calder March

A border region of six systems where Union space meets the Federation's and Prism's, on the inner edge of the middle ring. The Federation calls it the Calder March, after its naval station; Union crews call it the Seam. It matters, but only a little: a crossing point for ore going inward and goods coming out, policed, taxed and argued over, and not fought over since the Treaty was signed at Calder in 2397. All six systems are within a jump of each other. Lanes run in to the Federation core from Calder, in to Prism's inner systems from Serein, and out to the rest of the Red Ring from Brannock and Ostry. Beyond Ostry is the lane to Varrow, closed since 2524.

About 16 billion people live in the March, nearly half of them on Merrow. These are old, full systems, settled early in the Rush; every world with air has cities on it, and every lane is busy.

## Brannock (Union)

An orange star; Cinder, a scorched mining world; Halde, a cold, cloudy habitat world where most people live; the gas giant Vast and its refinery moon Tallow; Kettle Yard, a shipyard station; the Scree, a belt between Halde and Vast; Cinder Tailings, the spoil of the mines. Population about 4 billion: Halde 3.6 billion, in arcologies along its coasts and valleys; Tallow 300 million; Cinder 80 million, in tunnel towns on the night side; Kettle Yard 20 million.

Exports ore and refined metals inward through Calder; imports food from Merrow and components from Quire and Serein. Brannock's council sits on Halde and sends its delegates to the Plenum from there. The Federation inspects every ore hauler that leaves, and Brannock resents it. Blackhulls drink in its docks.

Since 2524: crews and families out of Varrow have come through Ostry and settled on Halde, some two million of them, in emptied towers and borrowed habs. Kettle Yard has the Plenum's order to refit haulers for the navy, and is slow to fill it.

## Ostry (Union)

A dim red dwarf; an ice moon round a gas giant, with towns cut into the ice; the giant skimmed for fuel; tugs, tanks and station rings. Population about 150 million, most of it under the ice. Poor, cold, tight-knit, and closer to the Blackhulls than the Plenum admits.

Exports water and fuel to the whole March; imports everything else. It was Varrow's nearest neighbour, and most of the people who got out of Varrow came through here. The Federation keeps a quarantine picket at Ostry's jump zone for the closed lane, and Ostry's people watch it the way you watch a dog that has bitten someone.

## Calder (Federation)

A white star; Calder, a dry, cold world with thin air, settled early in the Rush, its cities grown up round the navy; Calder Station in orbit, the navy's base for the March, with a picket squadron, customs and the levy office for Union cargo. The Treaty was signed here in 2397, in a room in the old naval yard that is now a museum nobody visits. Population about 900 million: the navy and its contractors, and everyone who works for them or sells to them.

Every Union cargo going inward passes Calder's inspections. The navy is thin here and getting thinner: ships and crews are being pulled to the Shadow front, and the officers left behind are junior, overworked and touchy. The Calder picket also supplies the Ostry quarantine, and its crews talk.

## Merrow (Federation)

A yellow star; Merrow itself, a green, wet world under farm domes and open fields, the only world in the March where food grows in the open; two small moons with agricultural stations. Population about 7.5 billion, nearly half the March's people: farm towns, food-processing cities, and the ports that ship it all out.

Exports food to every system in the March; imports machinery and medicine. Federal and comfortable and a little smug about it, and nervous lately: it has taken in Shadow-front evacuees from further out, and the levy for the war has gone up twice.

## Serein (Prism)

A yellow star; Serein, a terraformed world of white towers and managed gardens, Prism's company world for the March; a ring of orbital clinics. Population about 2.8 billion, Prism associates and their families, and the people who serve them. Clean, quiet, expensive, and polite to the point of menace.

Exports medicine, finance and credit; it holds the debts of half the Union crews in the March, which Prism may not do and the Union pretends not to know. Imports food and metals. Prism's statement on the 2524 quarantine was issued here, and the associates repeat it word for word.

## Quire (Prism)

A white star; a hot, barren inner world built over with navigation-core foundries; worker cities in domes on its night side and habitats in orbit. Population about 600 million, mostly foundry staff on fixed contracts and their families.

Exports navigation cores, the most valuable cargo in the March, under heavy guard. Imports metals and food. Quire was the sister plant of the foundry system that went glass in 2524. Since then its staff have been rotated out faster than usual, its output has dropped, and every core it ships goes inward under escort rather than to the March's own yards.

## Between them

- Ore and metals flow inward: Brannock to Calder, and on to the Federation core; some to Quire for the foundry.
- Food flows out from Merrow to everyone.
- Water and fuel flow from Ostry to everyone.
- Medicine and credit flow out from Serein; debt flows back.
- Navigation cores flow inward from Quire under escort, and the March's own yards go short.
- Varrow survivors flow from Ostry to Halde; Shadow-front evacuees reach Merrow from the Federation side.
- The navy flows outward, toward the front, and doesn't come back.
