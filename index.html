One Newtonian physics for every ship; assist is an autopilot on the same thrusters. Hydrogen drags and refuels you, dust blinds sensors, ionized gas eats shields. Radiation drains shields and raises sensor noise; planets cast shadows from it. Hostiles only engage what their sensors hold, so running cold in dust breaks contact.
